# 🛒 Save for Later Feature Implementation

### End-to-End Business Analysis Case Study

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Business Analysis case study focused on designing a **Save for Later** feature for an online grocery delivery platform.

The feature addresses a common customer problem: users may find products they want to purchase but are not ready to buy immediately. Without a way to retain those products, they may forget them, search for them again, or abandon the purchase journey.

The proposed solution allows customers to save products, manage their saved items, and move them to the cart when they are ready to purchase.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

The analysis identified several business challenges:

- Customer retention declined by **12%**
- Repeat purchases declined by **9%**
- Cart abandonment increased by **18%**
- Customers had no dedicated way to retain products for future purchase

### Objective

Design a feature that improves the shopping experience while creating opportunities to increase customer retention, repeat purchases, and purchase conversion.

---

## 🔎 What I Did

This project follows the feature through the complete Business Analysis lifecycle:

**Business Problem → Requirements → Process Analysis → UX Design → Validation → Data Analysis → Business Insights**

### Key Deliverables

- **Project Charter** — Project scope, objectives, stakeholders, risks and success metrics
- **BRD** — Business requirements and business rules
- **FRD** — Functional and non-functional requirements
- **User Stories** — User stories with acceptance criteria and priorities
- **Process Flows** — As-Is and To-Be customer journeys
- **Wireframes** — Proposed Save for Later user experience
- **UAT** — User acceptance scenarios
- **SQL Analysis** — Business questions, queries, KPIs and recommendations
- **Power BI Dashboard** — Visual analysis of business performance

---

## 📊 Key Findings

The SQL analysis was performed on a dataset containing:

| Dataset | Records |
|---|---:|
| Customers | 500 |
| Orders | 1,289 |
| Products | 96 |
| Saved Products | 3,000 |

Some key metrics identified through the analysis:

- **Total Revenue:** ₹538,404.35
- **Average Order Value:** ₹417.69
- **Save-to-Purchase Conversion:** ~42%
- Returning customers showed slightly higher Save-to-Purchase conversion than new customers.
- Several highly-saved products showed relatively low purchase conversion, highlighting opportunities to improve the feature's effectiveness.
